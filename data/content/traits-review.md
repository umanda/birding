## Doubts and items for the reviewer

**Possible errors in workbook prose. Not changed; listed for the owner:**
- **Crimson-backed Flameback:** `species_description` says "golden-yellow crown and crest". This
  conflicts with the same row's `male_female_identification` (male red crown, female black
  crown with white spots), which matches my knowledge. I did **not** tag yellow. Check against
  Warakagoda et al. 2012.
- **Purple-rumped Sunbird:** the description says male "glossy green crown and upperparts". In
  life the male's mantle and wing-coverts look dark maroon. I kept `green` (medium) and noted
  that `rufous` may be better.
- **Purple Sunbird** "greyish-brown lower belly" (existing flag F7): not used in colours.
- **Asian Koel juvenile:** the workbook says juveniles resemble the female (brown, spotted). I
  recall juveniles as largely blackish with pale spots. I kept the workbook value at medium.
- **Western Reef-Heron:** the workbook says winter migrant Oct–Apr (`M`). I believe birds are
  present year-round in the north-west (Mannar/Kalpitiya). This needs eBird bar charts and a CBC
  status check before the month rows are reviewed.

**Values that are mine, not the workbook's** (`source=expert:ornithologist`): aerial (Shikra,
Parakeet, Little Tern), perched_open (House Crow, Barn Swallow), ground (House Crow, Spotted
Dove), wading (Golden Plover, Cattle Egret), eclipse colours (Garganey, Purple Sunbird),
non-breeding and juvenile Little Tern colours, the AR/IN zone additions marked [X], and most
commonness ratings.

**Zone mapping:** the prose almost never names the six zones. "Throughout / widespread" was
mapped to WL, WM, IN, DL and AR, and to MO only where hill presence is likely (crow, myna,
bulbul, magpie-robin, Spotted Dove, Barn Swallow, Common Sandpiper). I left MO **off** for
Pond-heron, Koel, WT Kingfisher, Parakeet, Cattle Egret, Waterhen and Purple-rumped Sunbird. That is
a judgement call, so please check. Junglefowl in AR is low confidence: the workbook says "all
zones", but there is little forest on the Jaffna peninsula.

**Months:** Common Sandpiper May–Jul and Barn Swallow Jun–Aug were left out, although both
oversummer occasionally. Garganey September arrivals are possible. Little Tern is `all` per the
workbook, but numbers vary seasonally.

**Size edge cases:** Grey Hornbill (~45 cm) lands exactly on the XL/XXL boundary. Paradise-flycatcher
males look XL with streamers, but the length excludes streamers. Peafowl length is from Wikipedia
because the workbook has none (flag F2). Please confirm it against a field guide.

**Colour cap (3) hides some striking accents:** Red-wattled Lapwing red wattles, Common Myna white
wing patches, Red-vented Bulbul white rump, Waterhen red bill, Junglefowl dark tail. Consider
restoring §3's `main/accent` prominence in a later release.

**Test sightings:** t12 (female Koel) is deliberately hard: brown/white in a tree also fits a
juvenile Shikra. t20 (Shikra, size skipped) and t23 (bulbul, colours skipped) exercise the
commonness tie-break. t16 (Reef-Heron in December) depends on the month data above.

**Sensitive species:** Blue Magpie is on the §6 "obscure location" list. Rose-ringed Parakeet is on
the §6 trade-targeted list (no point locations). These files hold zone-level data only, which is
coarse enough. Nothing here records nest, roost or colony sites.
