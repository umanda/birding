Sri Lanka Birds — low-fidelity wireframes

Phase 1 (Discover). These are grey boxes only and are not a visual design. Each frame is 360×780. The flows, states and tokens are in 01-ux-flows.md. Occurrence badges always pair colour with a symbol, a letter and a word. Every frame shows the language toggle (සිං | EN). The sample content is in English UI mode. Photo ID is not in the MVP and appears only as a disabled "coming soon" card. There is no sign-in wall, and sign-in is offered only to back up the life list. Status values not supplied for this mock-up are shown as "[from data]" and are never invented. Frames 4a–4d (Identify) were updated for M5, spec docs/specs/004-step-by-step-id.md. They cover 5 questions with the month automatic, Not sure on every question, a sticky "See N matches", why lines and flags, the no-match state and the coverage footer. On the M5 Identify home (frame 1), only the Step-by-step button is in scope.

Notes: the frames show no colour other than the badges, the primary button and the 12 colour swatches in 4b. The swatch hues are placeholders. The final hues come with the reviewed colour glossary, and every swatch always keeps its text name. Sinhala answer labels are not drawn, because they come from the owner-reviewed glossary (spec 004, Data). The Sinhala UI string on the sign-in frame is a placeholder and needs review by a native speaker before use. Species names are taken from the brief and must be checked against the workbook. Photo credit lines are format placeholders only.
