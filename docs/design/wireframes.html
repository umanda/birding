Sri Lanka Birds — low-fidelity wireframes

Phase 1 (Discover). These are grey boxes only and are not a visual design. Each frame is 360×780. The flows, states and tokens are in 01-ux-flows.md. Occurrence badges always pair colour with a symbol, a letter and a word. Every frame shows the language toggle (සිං | EN). The sample content is in English UI mode. Photo ID is not in the MVP and appears only as a disabled "coming soon" card. There is no sign-in wall, and sign-in is offered only to back up the life list. Status values not supplied for this mock-up are shown as "[from data]" and are never invented.

Notes: the frames show no colour other than the badges and the primary button. The Sinhala UI string on the sign-in frame is a placeholder and needs review by a native speaker before use. Species names are taken from the brief and must be checked against the workbook. Photo credit lines are format placeholders only.
